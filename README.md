Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521) competition "One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge" ($3, top 3 paid $1 each).

## The line

> My fridge keeps a tub of something from 2021 like it's a family heirloom, and the smell has seniority over me now.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), with a 1–200 char gate check and a
read-back verification. The roast targets one specific, recognisable fridge
detail — the mystery tub nobody dares throw away — and turns on the twist in
the last words: the smell has seniority over the owner.

Run it locally:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/mftest-fridge python3 main.py
```
